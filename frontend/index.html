Polymarket hourly Bitcoin prediction markets

This page describes a free-to-use sample dataset of hourly Bitcoin “Up or Down” prediction markets from Polymarket. The dataset combines Polymarket market metadata and order-book observations with BTCUSDT prices from Binance.

Dataset metadata

  • Dataset name: Polymarket hourly Bitcoin prediction markets
  • Version: 1.0
  • Author: KristijanGas
  • License: CC0 1.0 Universal
  • Data type: time-series and prediction-market data
  • Covered period: 5 July 2026, 05:00-13:00 UTC
  • Market outcomes: Up and Down
  • Sampling interval: approximately one observation per second

Dataset contents

Each record contains the trading date, hourly market interval, Polymarket asset and outcome, observation timestamp, BTCUSDT price, and the best bid and ask prices. Individual order-book levels and quantities are not included.

Machine-readable data

The dataset is available in CSV and JSON formats. The structure of the JSON data is described by schema.json.
